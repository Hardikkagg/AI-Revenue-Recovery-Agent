================================================================================
AI REVENUE RECOVERY AGENT — SYSTEM & OPERATIONAL MODEL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern digital businesses lose between 5% to 15% of their total potential
revenue to failed transactions, involuntary subscriber churn, and checkout drop-
offs. Traditional recovery methods rely on rigid, static rules (e.g., blanket
retries every 24 hours or generic automated emails) that fail to adapt to
specific failure modes, resulting in customer frustration, processor fraud flags,
and permanently lost revenue.


2. THE REVENUE-LOSS PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Revenue leakage occurs primarily across three critical friction points:
- Involuntary payment decline: Transitory network errors, processor timeouts,
  or temporary insufficient funds that fail when retried naively or too frequently.
- Checkout cart abandonment: High-intent customers dropping off due to unexpected
  fees, payment-form friction, or checkout hesitations.
- Involuntary subscription churn: Expired credit cards, billing cycle shifts, or
  unresponsive dunning sequences leading to silent customer loss.


3. PROPOSED SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An autonomous, intelligent Revenue Recovery Agent that replaces static rules
with a hybrid decision-making pipeline. The system detects risk events in real
time, diagnoses root causes, assesses recovery probability, selects high-ROI
personalized recovery strategies, executes safe simulated interventions,
observes the outcome, and adapts its policy to maximize lifetime customer value
and recovered revenue.


4. WHAT THE AI REVENUE RECOVERY AGENT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AI Revenue Recovery Agent is a specialized hybrid autonomous agent. It is
not merely an LLM prompt or a standard predictive model; it is an integrated
system combining:
- Deterministic rules for fast, safety-critical bounds and guardrails
- Machine learning for empirical probability estimation
- Generative language models for contextual reasoning and communication
- Adaptive learning (reinforcement learning / bandits) for dynamic timing
- Simulation layers for verified, safe operational execution
- Persistent state management for auditable financial tracking


5. THREE PRIMARY RECOVERY SCENARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario A: Payment Failure
- Characteristics: E-commerce or one-off transaction declined by gateway.
- Failure types: `network_error`, `processor_timeout`, `insufficient_funds`,
  `card_expired`, `card_declined`, `fraud_hold`, `session_timeout`.
- Target strategies: `retry_now` (immediate transient fix), `retry_later` (timed
  smart delay), `request_alternate_payment`, `escalate_to_manual_review`.

Scenario B: Checkout Abandonment
- Characteristics: User reached checkout stage but left before payment completion.
- Failure types: `payment_form_dropoff`, `shipping_cost`, `comparison_shopping`,
  `cart_hesitation`, `unspecified`.
- Target strategies: `send_checkout_reminder`, `offer_discount` (future), `do_nothing`.

Scenario C: Subscription Failure
- Characteristics: Recurring billing renewal failed for an existing subscriber.
- Failure types: `card_expired`, `dunning_unresponsive`, `insufficient_funds`,
  `account_closed`, `plan_cancelled_intent`.
- Target strategies: `send_subscription_update_request`, `retry_later`,
  `escalate_to_manual_review`.


6. COMPLETE AGENTIC WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The end-to-end lifecycle follows 9 distinct stages:

  [1. DETECT]
       Standardizes and validates incoming transaction/event payloads.
       ↓
  [2. DIAGNOSE]
       Evaluates error codes and history to identify root causes and recoverability.
       ↓
  [3. SCORE]
       Estimates recovery probability [0.0 - 1.0] and assigns confidence levels.
       ↓
  [4. CHOOSE STRATEGY]
       Selects optimal action from 7 distinct strategies based on diagnosis and score.
       ↓
  [5. GENERATE ACTION]
       Drafts personalized customer communications and schedules execution parameters.
       ↓
  [6. EXECUTE]
       Dispatches simulated retries, dunning notifications, or manual escalation tasks.
       ↓
  [7. OBSERVE OUTCOME]
       Tracks transaction response (success, failure, customer interaction, timeout).
       ↓
  [8. UPDATE LEARNING]
       Adjusts model weights and bandit action values based on observed outcome.
       ↓
  [9. MEASURE REVENUE RECOVERED]
       Records recovered dollars, ROI metrics, and operational performance.


7. ROLE OF DETERMINISTIC RULES / LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deterministic logic enforces hard business boundaries and safety constraints:
- Immediate isolation of fraud holds (`fraud_hold`) and closed accounts.
- Event payload schema validation and constraint normalization.
- Baseline fallback recovery routing when confidence is low.
- Guaranteed compliance with gateway retry frequency restrictions.


8. ROLE OF MACHINE LEARNING (ML)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Supervised statistical models (e.g., scikit-learn Logistic Regression / Gradient
Boosting) predict recovery probability from tabular historical features:
- Customer account age, historical recovery rate, past failures vs successes.
- Event amount, payment method category, failure code historical recovery likelihood.
- Provides calibrated continuous probability scores rather than heuristic guesses.


9. ROLE OF LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS (LLM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The LLM (Ollama / Local LLM) operates within bounded supervisory roles:
- Natural-language reasoning summaries explaining *why* an action was chosen.
- Dynamic generation of personalized, non-generic customer outreach messages
  (email, SMS) tailored to tone, failure type, and customer history.
- The LLM does NOT blindly control financial transactions directly without logic
  guardrails.


10. ROLE OF LEARNING SYSTEM (MULTI-ARMED BANDIT / RL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The adaptive learning layer optimizes dynamic parameters over time:
- Selecting optimal retry delays (e.g., +4 hours vs +24 hours vs +72 hours).
- Channel selection optimization (email vs SMS vs in-app nudge).
- Balances exploration of new recovery timings with exploitation of proven paths.


11. ROLE OF THE SIMULATION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enables realistic, safe demonstrations and testing without live banking APIs:
- Simulates realistic payment gateway responses based on statistical failure rates.
- Simulates customer behavioral responses (e.g., clicking a reminder link or
  updating an expired card).
- Provides deterministic repeatable states for evaluation.


12. ROLE OF THE DATABASE (SQLite + SQLAlchemy)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provides persistent state, auditable logs, and reporting data:
- Tables: `customers`, `recovery_cases`, `events`, `actions`.
- Tracks the full history of every case across all 9 stages.
- Powers the real-time financial metrics and analytics dashboard.


13. WHAT MAKES THE SYSTEM AGENTIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system is genuinely agentic because:
- Autonomous perception-to-action loop: It ingests external stimuli, perceives
  context, deliberates across multiple modules (rules + ML + LLM), and decides
  actions autonomously.
- Goal-directed behavior: Its primary objective is maximizing recovered revenue
  while minimizing customer churn and processing friction.
- Closed-loop feedback: It does not stop at outputting recommendations; it
  observes the actual results of its actions and learns from experience.
